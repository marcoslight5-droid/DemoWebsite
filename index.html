Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Everything you need to know about our design process and services

How long does a typical interior design project take? ⏱️

The timeline varies based on project scope, but most residential projects take between 3-6 months from concept to completion. Commercial projects may take longer depending on size and complexity.

Do you work with clients on a budget? 💰

Absolutely! We believe great design should be accessible. We work closely with clients to establish a realistic budget and provide creative solutions that maximize impact while respecting financial constraints.

What's your design process? 🎯

Our process begins with an in-depth consultation to understand your vision, lifestyle, and preferences. We then develop concepts, create detailed plans, source materials, and manage the entire implementation process.

Do you handle renovations or only new spaces? 🔨

We specialize in both! Whether you're starting from scratch or transforming an existing space, we have the expertise to handle renovations, remodels, and new construction projects.

Can you work with my existing furniture? 🛋️

Yes! We love incorporating meaningful pieces into our designs. During our consultation, we'll assess your existing furniture and determine how to best integrate it with the new design concept.
